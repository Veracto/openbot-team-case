# Building a practical agent team

## The problem

A small organization has recurring work across research, content, growth and relationship management. A single general-purpose chat can help with individual requests, but ownership becomes unclear when research, drafting and follow-through cross responsibilities. We wanted clearer ownership, useful handoffs and a reviewable outcome while keeping access appropriate to the task.

## Design decisions

1. **Roles follow responsibilities.** Content Lead owns the content flow and coordinates research, production and growth analysis. CRM manages a separate area of work.
2. **A known task goes to its owner.** Work does not need a universal router or a new background system simply because more than one agent exists. Shared discussions and cross-team coordination are used when the task calls for them.
3. **People retain decisions.** Agents produce material and carry out scoped tasks. A person remains responsible for business judgment and publication.
4. **Access is part of the operating model.** The company team uses defined company resources and tools; unrelated private contexts and administrative capabilities are outside its scope. A team-member interface and an agent's tool permissions are separate boundaries.
5. **Evidence precedes claims.** A successful message, tool call or permission test is useful evidence for that behavior; it does not by itself prove a complete business workflow or its impact.

## Roles and handoffs

People & Performance supports role requirements and assessment work, without implying that a real employee assessment has been conducted. Meeting Notes serves personal and work intake; when relevant, it can pass meeting context directly to CRM for follow-up. Chief of Staff coordinates work across roles, while a known task can still go directly to its responsible owner. The working diagram does not itself grant access to agent conversations or company resources.

## A verified coordination example

During the 4 November 2026 access acceptance, the native company Chief of Staff → CRM Manager → Chief of Staff transport was tested and the returned result reconciled. The same acceptance exercised company-resource operations and negative-access checks. This is an operational handoff test, not a claim that a real customer was contacted or that a sales outcome occurred.

## A content example to develop

A content owner receives an objective, asks research to gather relevant evidence, coordinates production of a draft, and returns a reviewable result. The human can then decide whether to publish or request changes. This is an illustrative workflow based on role responsibilities; its end-to-end completion and impact are not asserted here.

## Current boundary and development

The six-role company configuration and scoped tests were recorded on 4 November 2026. At that point the teammate interface had been exercised using an ephemeral member; a real teammate-device login was a separate outstanding acceptance step. This dated evidence is not a live dashboard of today's configuration.

Next steps are to document individual end-to-end episodes with suitable evidence, check outcomes with the people involved, and update the case when the operating setup materially changes. We will not publish private customer records, internal conversations, credentials or agent instruction files as evidence.
